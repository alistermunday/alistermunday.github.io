🍌 One Large Ripe Banana → Cooked, Not Raw

1. Pan-Fried Caramelized Banana

5 min

Slice lengthwise or into rounds. Melt a knob of butter in a pan, add a spoon of sugar (brown if you have it), fry 2 min a side until golden and syrupy. Dash of cinnamon at the end. Eat straight from the pan or over yogurt/ice cream.

2. Banana Flambé

5 min

Same as above but once caramelized, add a splash of dark rum, tilt the pan, light it (carefully) or just let it bubble off. Instant dessert, feels ridiculous for one banana.

3. Grilled / Griddled Banana Boats

10 min

Halve lengthwise (skin on or off). Score the flesh, stuff with chocolate chunks + a pinch of salt. Onto a hot pan or under the broiler until the chocolate melts and the banana softens.

4. Banana Fritters — full method

15 min · makes ~8 small fritters

Ingredients:
1 large ripe banana (~150g peeled), mashed
75g self-raising flour (or 75g plain flour + ¼ tsp / 1.5g baking powder)
50ml milk
15g caster sugar (1 tbsp)
pinch of salt
¼ tsp ground cinnamon (optional)
30ml vegetable oil, for frying

Method:
1. Mash the banana in a bowl — a few lumps are fine.
2. Stir in the flour, sugar, salt, and cinnamon.
3. Pour in the milk gradually, mixing to a thick, dollop-able batter (not runny).
4. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
5. Drop in ~2 tbsp of batter per fritter, flattening slightly with the back of the spoon.
6. Fry ~2 min a side until golden and cooked through.
7. Drain on paper towel, dust with extra sugar/cinnamon if you like, serve warm.

5. Stir Into Hot Oatmeal / Porridge

5 min

Mash half the banana into the porridge while cooking so it melts in and sweetens it, slice the rest on top caramelized from the pan-fry method above.

6. Banana Curry (savoury, if feeling bold)

15 min

Sri Lankan-style: chunk the banana, simmer briefly in coconut milk with curry leaves/turmeric/mustard seed if you have them, chili, a squeeze of lime. Sweet-savoury, good over rice.
